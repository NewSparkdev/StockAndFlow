Privacy Policy

App: Stock & Flow
Developer: NewSpark
Effective Date: July 18, 2026

1. Overview

Stock & Flow is a business management app for inventory, sales, and expense tracking. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes how the app handles your information.

2. Data We Collect

Stock & Flow does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with us or any third party. All data you enter into the app — including inventory items, sales records, expense records, customer information, and business settings — is stored only on your device.

3. Data Storage

All app data is stored locally in a database on your device. We do not operate servers, cloud services, or remote databases in connection with this app. Data does not leave your device unless you explicitly use the Export feature, which saves a file to your chosen location.

4. Internet Access

Stock & Flow does not require an internet connection to function and does not make network requests during normal use.

5. Permissions

The app may request the following Android permissions:

  • Camera — used only for the barcode scanner feature to scan product barcodes. No images are saved or transmitted.
  • Storage / Files — used only when you choose to export data or save a PDF invoice to your device.

6. Third-Party Services

Stock & Flow does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
