GOOGLE PLAY — CONTENT RATING QUESTIONNAIRE ANSWERS
====================================================

When Play Store asks these questions, answer as follows:

VIOLENCE
- Does your app contain references to or depictions of violence? NO

SEXUAL CONTENT
- Does your app contain sexual content? NO

LANGUAGE
- Does your app contain profanity or crude humor? NO

CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES
- Does your app contain references to drugs, alcohol, or tobacco? NO

USER GENERATED CONTENT
- Does your app allow users to post content that other users can see? NO

LOCATION SHARING
- Does your app share the user's location with other users? NO

PERSONAL INFORMATION SHARING
- Does your app collect personal information?
  → Select "No, I do not collect personal information"

→ Final rating will be: EVERYONE
