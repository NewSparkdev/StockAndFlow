FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 chars)
----------------------------------
Stock & Flow is the all-in-one business management app built for small business owners who need real results without the complexity.

Track your inventory, record sales, log expenses, and view profit & loss reports — all from your phone.

INVENTORY MANAGEMENT
• Add, edit, and search stock items
• Track quantity, cost, and sale price per item
• Barcode scanner support for fast lookup
• Low stock alerts so you never run out

SALES
• Record sales in seconds
• Attach sales to customers for history tracking
• Automatic profit calculation on every transaction
• Generate PDF invoices directly from the app

EXPENSES
• Log business expenses by category
• Date-range filtering to review spending periods
• Tracks total expenses against revenue for accurate P&L

REPORTS & INSIGHTS
• Real-time dashboard: revenue, net profit, gross profit, inventory value
• Detailed P&L report with sales breakdown
• Sales trend tracking over any date range
• Export data for your accountant or records

CUSTOMERS
• Maintain a customer list with contact info
• Attach purchases to customers for relationship tracking

BUSINESS SETTINGS
• Set your business name, address, tax rate, and contact info
• Business info automatically fills into generated invoices

WORKS OFFLINE
• No internet connection required — your data stays on your device
• No subscription fees, no monthly charges

Stock & Flow is designed for retail shops, resellers, market vendors, freelancers, and any small business owner who needs a fast, reliable way to stay on top of their numbers.
