# Stock & Flow - Business Plan
**Inventory Management Software for Small Businesses**

---

## Executive Summary

### Business Overview
Stock & Flow is a desktop inventory management application designed for small to medium-sized businesses that need powerful inventory tracking without the complexity and cost of enterprise solutions. The software provides real-time inventory tracking, sales recording, financial reporting, and Shopify integration.

### Value Proposition
- **Simple & Affordable** - One-time purchase or low monthly subscription vs. expensive enterprise solutions
- **Shopify Integration** - Automatic sync with Shopify stores (major competitive advantage)
- **Fast & Reliable** - Handles 10,000+ records with sub-10ms performance
- **Desktop-First** - No internet required for core functionality, data stays on user's computer
- **Business Intelligence** - Real-time profit margins, inventory valuation, and trend analysis

### Target Market
Small to medium businesses with 100-10,000 sales per month:
- Shopify store owners
- Retail shops (boutiques, hardware stores, specialty shops)
- Wholesale distributors
- Small manufacturers
- Pop-up and market vendors

### Business Model Options
**Option 1: One-Time Purchase** - $299-$499 per license
**Option 2: Subscription** - $29-$49/month
**Option 3: Freemium** - Free basic version, $79/month for Pro features

### Financial Projections (Year 1)
- **Conservative:** 50 customers × $39/month = $23,400/year
- **Moderate:** 200 customers × $39/month = $93,600/year
- **Optimistic:** 500 customers × $39/month = $234,000/year

## Business Model & Pricing Strategy

### Recommended: Hybrid Model

#### **Tier 1: Free Edition** ($0)
**Purpose:** Lead generation, market penetration
**Limits:**
- Up to 100 inventory items
- Up to 500 sales per year
- Basic reporting only
- No Shopify integration
- Community support only

**Target:** Hobby sellers, market testing, students

#### **Tier 2: Pro Edition** ($39/month or $399/year)
**Purpose:** Core revenue driver
**Features:**
- Unlimited inventory items
- Unlimited sales
- Shopify integration
- Advanced reporting
- Email support
- Excel import/export
- All future updates

**Target:** Small businesses, Shopify sellers, growing retailers

#### **Tier 3: Business Edition** ($79/month or $799/year)
**Purpose:** High-value customers
**Features:**
- Everything in Pro
- Multi-store support (up to 5 locations)
- Priority support
- Custom reports
- API access (future)
- White-label option (future)

**Target:** Multi-location retailers, larger businesses

#### **One-Time Purchase** ($499 - Lifetime Pro License)
**Purpose:** Cash flow, early adopters
**Features:**
- Pro Edition features
- Lifetime updates (version 1.x)
- No monthly fees
- One computer license

**Target:** Budget-conscious businesses, anti-subscription customers

### Pricing Psychology
- **$39/month** - Sweet spot between "too cheap to be good" and "too expensive"
- **Annual discount** - $399/year saves $69 (15% discount) = stronger cash flow
- **Lifetime option** - Pays for itself in 13 months, attracts committed users
- **Free tier** - Massive market penetration, converts 2-5% to paid

## Risk Analysis & Mitigation

### Technical Risks

**Risk: Software bugs causing data loss**
- *Mitigation:* Automatic backups, comprehensive testing, version control
- *Severity:* High | *Likelihood:* Low

**Risk: Performance issues at scale**
- *Mitigation:* Already tested with 10K records, database optimizations in place
- *Severity:* Medium | *Likelihood:* Low

**Risk: Windows-only limits market**
- *Mitigation:* 85% of small businesses use Windows; Mac version in roadmap
- *Severity:* Medium | *Likelihood:* Medium

### Market Risks

**Risk: Shopify changes API, breaks integration**
- *Mitigation:* Follow Shopify developer guidelines, maintain API version compatibility
- *Severity:* High | *Likelihood:* Low

**Risk: Large competitor targets same niche**
- *Mitigation:* Move fast, build loyal community, focus on superior UX
- *Severity:* High | *Likelihood:* Medium

**Risk: Market doesn't want desktop software**
- *Mitigation:* Free tier validates demand before heavy investment; pivot to web if needed
- *Severity:* High | *Likelihood:* Low

### Business Risks

**Risk: Can't acquire customers profitably**
- *Mitigation:* Focus on organic channels (SEO, content), leverage Shopify app store
- *Severity:* High | *Likelihood:* Medium

**Risk: High churn rate (customers cancel quickly)**
- *Mitigation:* Excellent onboarding, quick support response, regular feature updates
- *Severity:* High | *Likelihood:* Medium

**Risk: One-person operation, can't scale support**
- *Mitigation:* Hire part-time support at 100 customers, build help center early
- *Severity:* Medium | *Likelihood:* High
